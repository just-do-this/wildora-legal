Wildora Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 29, 2026 · Contact: funnychen@outlook.com

Wildora (“小旷野”) is a personal wishlist and dreams app for iPhone. This policy describes how the app handles information.

Summary

  • No account, username, or password is required.
  • Your wishes, lists, notes, photos, and settings are stored on your iPhone by default.
  • Wildora Pro can optionally sync data through your private iCloud account when you turn sync on.
  • We do not run our own user database, sell personal data, or use advertising tracking.

Information stored on your device

The app stores the content you create: nicknames, optional profile photos, wish lists, wishes, notes, note photos, dreams, energy rewards, and app preferences (appearance, language, sync toggle, and similar settings).

iCloud sync (Pro, optional)

If you subscribe to Wildora Pro and enable iCloud sync, your app data is stored in your private CloudKit container (iCloud.com.barrychen.wildora) and synced between devices signed into the same iCloud account. We do not receive direct access to your iCloud data.

Purchases

Membership purchases and restores are processed by Apple through StoreKit. We receive only the entitlement information needed to unlock Pro features. Payment details are handled by Apple.

Photos

You may attach photos to notes and set a profile photo using the system photo picker. If you save a share image, the app requests add-only access to your photo library to save that image.

Network use

The app may download public list templates from our object storage over HTTPS. These requests do not include your personal wish content. Optional links in templates or settings may open Safari or other apps you choose.

What we do not collect

  • No analytics SDKs, advertising identifiers, or cross-app tracking.
  • No server-side account profile tied to your identity.

Children

Wildora is not directed at children under 13 and is not a “Made for Kids” app.

Retention and deletion

Delete content inside the app, uninstall the app to remove on-device data, or manage iCloud data through your Apple ID settings when sync is enabled.

Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above will change when we do.

小旷野隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 8 月 29 日 · 联系：funnychen@outlook.com

小旷野（Wildora）是一款个人 iPhone 心愿与梦想记录应用。本政策说明应用如何处理信息。

概要

  • 无需注册账号、用户名或密码。
  • 你的心愿、清单、笔记、照片和设置默认保存在本机。
  • Wildora Pro 可在您开启同步后，通过您的私人 iCloud 账号可选同步。
  • 我们不运营独立用户数据库，不出售个人数据，也不做广告追踪。

保存在您设备上的信息

应用保存您创建的内容：昵称、可选头像、心愿清单、事项、笔记、笔记照片、梦想、能量奖励，以及外观、语言、同步开关等偏好设置。

iCloud 同步（Pro，可选）

若您订阅 Wildora Pro 并开启 iCloud 同步，数据会存入您的私人 CloudKit 容器（iCloud.com.barrychen.wildora），并在登录同一 iCloud 账号的设备间同步。我们无法直接访问您的 iCloud 数据。

购买

会员购买与恢复购买由 Apple 通过 StoreKit 处理。我们仅接收解锁 Pro 功能所需的权益信息，支付细节由 Apple 处理。

照片

您可通过系统照片选择器为笔记添加照片或设置头像。保存分享图时，应用会请求“仅添加”相册权限以保存该图片。

网络访问

应用可能通过 HTTPS 下载公开的清单模板 JSON，这些请求不包含您的个人心愿内容。模板或设置中的可选链接可能在 Safari 或其他您选择的应用中打开。

我们不收集的内容

  • 不使用分析 SDK、广告标识符或跨应用追踪。
  • 不建立与您身份绑定的服务端账号档案。

儿童

小旷野并非面向 13 岁以下儿童，也不是“儿童类别”应用。

保留与删除

可在应用内删除内容；卸载应用可移除本机数据；开启同步后可通过 Apple ID 设置管理 iCloud 数据。

变更

我们可能更新本政策，届时会修改上方生效日期。
