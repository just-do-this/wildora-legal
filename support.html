Membership

Wildora Air is free. Wildora Pro adds higher quotas and optional iCloud sync. Manage or restore purchases in the app under Settings → membership, or in your Apple ID subscription settings.

iCloud sync

Sync is optional, Pro-only, and off by default. Turn it on in Settings when you want lists and notes on this device to follow your iCloud account.
